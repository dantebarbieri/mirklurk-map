My worlds & sync

    Other libraries saved in this browser

      This browser remembers your library. Sync your other PCs and your phone to see and update the same worlds; share a single world read-only with friends. Adding a world uploads only the selected character; adding one that is already in your library updates it instead. On a phone's Home Screen app, links open in the browser instead, so type the one-time code from Sync another device..., or paste a link, under Sync code or link.

      See your Mirklurk world: every zone by its in-game name, real game tiles and scenery for each saved zone, entrances, NPCs and loot — and where Fort Solid, Ranger Bhato, the Library, Scaal, Gurb-Gurb and Ihar are, or can still appear.

      The original map view is shown by default. Switch on Realistic mode to explore game tiles, from individual pixels out to the whole world.

      Pick your character's folder from the game's Saves folder — for example …\steamapps\common\Mirklurk Every Step Matters\Saves\YourName — or drop it anywhere on this page. Picking the whole Saves folder, or a .zip of it, works too.

      Your files stay in this browser unless you add a world to your library. Save and quit to the menu first so the files are current.

      Or opt into Live saves in desktop Chrome/Edge: choose the folder once for read-only monitoring. The map refreshes after the game saves, not every movement. Stop monitoring at any time. Saved items and loot can be inspected; wiki links open separately without uploading your saves.
