Change a Mirklurk character: skill points, items and coins (with their durability, fuel and wetness), where you stand, the time and weather, creatures, your level, hit points, wellbeing, satiation, focus, stamina and temperature. Viewing a world is MirkMap's job; this page changes the save files themselves.

Any saved edit marks the world as Cheated, permanently. MirkMap then shows it with a C badge on this device, in your synced library and to anyone you share it with. You will be asked to confirm before editing.

Save and quit to the game's main menu first, and stay at the menu until you have saved your edits here: the game keeps your character in memory and would overwrite them. Choose your character's folder from the game's Saves folder, for example …\steamapps\common\Mirklurk Every Step Matters\Saves\YourName, or the whole Saves folder. You can keep this page open while you play: save and quit to the menu again, then choose Reload save to edit the newest save, with your unsaved changes kept.

Editing needs a desktop browser that can write to a folder you choose (Chrome or Edge). Your files stay on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

Edits happen in this browser tab and are written only to the folder you choose. Before each save, the files it replaces are kept in this browser, so you can restore them.
